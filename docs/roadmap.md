# Roadmap

The first beta focuses on reliable self-hosting and the Form 1 flight-record workflow. Priorities may change based on pilot feedback; these are directions, not delivery commitments.

1. Collect redacted aircraft/controller/firmware compatibility reports and strengthen parser fixtures.
2. Review Japanese terminology and exported layouts with pilots who use the MLIT forms.
3. Improve data lifecycle controls: account recovery, deletion, retention, and backup ergonomics.
4. Evaluate daily inspection and maintenance records (Forms 2 and 3) as separate, explicitly scoped features.
5. Improve import diagnostics, accessibility, and mobile field use.

Public multi-tenant hosting, flight-permission decisions, and automated DIPS submissions are outside the current release scope.
