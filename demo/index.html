01 / IMPORT

Less copying.
More flying.

Import DJI flight records or add a flight manually. Encrypted logs need your own DJI API key; manual entries work without one.

02 / REVIEW

The pilot
has the final say.

Check flight details and suggested conditions, then sign. Signed entries lock until the signature is removed.

03 / KEEP

Your server.
Your fleet’s records.

Self-host with Docker. Export bilingual PDF and Excel records, and back up your database and original logs together.

BUILT FOR A SPECIFIC JOB

Flying in Japan is complicated.
Your logbook can be simpler.

Hikō is an independent project. It currently covers MLIT Form 1 flight records. Daily inspection and maintenance forms, permits, and DIPS submissions are separate.
