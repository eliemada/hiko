# Community conduct

Help make Hikō useful and welcoming to pilots with different backgrounds, languages, and levels of experience.

- Be respectful and specific. Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Help others reproduce issues and explain assumptions patiently.
- Respect privacy. Never publish someone's flight records, location, signature, contact details, or credentials without permission.
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate and consistent. Contact the [maintainer](https://github.com/eliemada) privately for sensitive conduct concerns; security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces operated for the project.
